# Power BI Blueprint

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
Four domain tiles with KPI cards, period selector, business-unit selector and alert strip.

## Page 2 — Customer Value
Segment treemap, LTV distribution, revenue concentration, customer table and segment drill-through.

## Page 3 — Workforce
Headcount, attrition, tenure bands, department comparison, overtime and satisfaction matrix.

## Page 4 — Finance
Revenue/profit trend, gross-to-operating margin bridge, expense composition and monthly variance.

## Page 5 — Fund Comparison
Return vs volatility scatter, drawdown comparison, risk-adjusted metric table and period controls.

## Modeling Principles
Use separate dimension tables for Date, Customer, Employee, Department and Fund where source data permits. Keep measures centralized and use consistent naming.

Important: fund metrics should include the measurement period and methodology in production; the sample values are illustrative.
