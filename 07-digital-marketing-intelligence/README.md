# 07 — Digital Marketing Intelligence

An integrated digital marketing analytics project connecting **SEO optimization, Google Ads simulation, and a 3-month content calendar** into one measurable campaign operating system.

## 01 — SEO Optimization
Audits blog and website pages across search intent, title/meta quality, content structure, internal linking, keyword coverage and technical opportunities.

## 02 — Google Ads Simulation
Models campaign performance using impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, spend, conversions, conversion rate and ROAS. The simulation separates traffic acquisition from conversion efficiency.

## 03 — 3-Month Content Calendar
Creates a structured publishing system across content pillars, formats, funnel stages, channels and campaign objectives.

## Measurement Framework

Awareness → Traffic → Engagement → Conversion → Revenue

### SEO
Impressions → Clicks → CTR → Landing sessions → Conversion

### Paid Search
Impressions → Clicks → Spend → Conversions → Revenue → ROAS

### Content
Content pieces → Reach → Engagement → Traffic → Assisted conversion

## Analytical Questions
- Which pages have the largest SEO opportunity?
- Which paid campaigns combine efficient traffic with conversion volume?
- Which content pillars are underrepresented across the funnel?
- How should content and paid media be measured together?
- Where does performance drop between click and conversion?
