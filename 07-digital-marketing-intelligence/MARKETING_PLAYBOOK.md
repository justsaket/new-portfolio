# Digital Marketing Measurement Playbook

## SEO
Prioritize pages using a combination of search opportunity, SEO quality score, engagement and conversion evidence. Avoid treating keyword volume alone as success.

## Google Ads
Track CTR for message/creative relevance, CPC for traffic cost, conversion rate for landing-page and audience efficiency, and ROAS for revenue efficiency.

## Content
Use the 3-month calendar to maintain balance across awareness, consideration and decision stages. Every content asset should have a defined objective and measurement path.

## Optimization Loop
Measure → Diagnose → Hypothesize → Change → Re-measure.

## Portfolio Note
The Ads data is a simulation and should not be represented as real campaign results. SEO scores are illustrative audit values.
